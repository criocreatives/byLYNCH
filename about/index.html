Romario Lynch, also known as LYNCH, is a self-taught photographer and artist from St. Elizabeth, Jamaica. He has been honing his skills since 2016 and has gained a reputation for his moody, saturated night photography and his keen eye for the female form. His work has been featured in notable publications such as The Fader, Noisey, Vice, Billboard, Global Grind and Carib Voxx. He currently resides in Kingston and continues to push the boundaries of his art and photography.
