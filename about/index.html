01 THE STORY

I saw it.
Now you get to.

Romario Lynch, also known as LYNCH, is a self-taught photographer and artist from St. Elizabeth, Jamaica. He first developed an interest in making images with camera phones in high school, before getting his first dedicated camera in 2014. He has been honing his skills since 2016, developing a reputation for moody, saturated night photography, dark and contrasted visuals, and a keen eye for the female form.

Though his photography spans portraiture, music and Jamaican culture, conceptual shoots and unguarded everyday moments, LYNCH thinks of himself first as a documentarian. Much of the work is simply a record of his own life—what he sees, who he meets and the moments he wants to remember. Instagram is where he shares that ongoing visual diary.

He also genuinely enjoys the aesthetics of making an image. The compositions, the colours, the contrast, the mood—and the process of turning an ordinary moment into a photograph that looks and feels right. That pleasure in making pictures is just as central to byLYNCH as the moments being documented.

His work has been featured in notable publications including The Fader, Noisey, Vice, Billboard, Global Grind and Carib Voxx. He currently resides in Kingston and continues to push the boundaries of his art and photography.

03 THE APPROACH

Moments worth
holding on to.

In a 2019 interview with Carib Voxx, LYNCH traced his interest in photography to the feeling of “freezing time”. He spoke about candid photography as a way of capturing a person's true self, and about using darker, contrasted visuals for conceptual work.

“I just document my life and share on Instagram.”

— LYNCH, on what the work is really about

There's no need to make it more complicated than that. It's the everyday seen through a particular eye: real people and real moments, with an appreciation for the beauty of a carefully made photograph. The archive becomes part personal diary, part study of colour, light, composition and the people around him.
