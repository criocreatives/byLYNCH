01 THE STORY

I saw it.
Now you get to.

Romario Lynch, also known as LYNCH, is a self-taught photographer and artist from St. Elizabeth, Jamaica. He first developed an interest in making images with camera phones in high school, before getting his first dedicated camera in 2014. He has been honing his skills since 2016, developing a reputation for moody, saturated night photography, dark and contrasted visuals, and a keen eye for the female form.

His practice spans portraiture, music and Jamaican culture, intimate conceptual photo sets and unguarded everyday moments. While much of the work is shaped by colour, atmosphere and shadow, candid photography remains just as important: noticing the details people might otherwise overlook.

His work has been featured in notable publications including The Fader, Noisey, Vice, Billboard, Global Grind and Carib Voxx. He currently resides in Kingston and continues to push the boundaries of his art and photography.

03 THE APPROACH

Moments worth
holding on to.

In a 2019 interview with Carib Voxx, LYNCH traced his interest in photography to the feeling of “freezing time”. His candid work seeks to show people as they really are; the conceptual work makes room for contrast, mood and creative experimentation.

“Capturing a person’s true self.”

— LYNCH, Carib Voxx interview (2019)

The result is an evolving visual archive of Jamaica—its faces, artists, communities and small, passing scenes—alongside a more personal exploration of beauty and the human form.
