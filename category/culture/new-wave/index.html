NEW WAVE

NEW WAVE is the brainchild of Protojé & Jason Panton. A platform for “New and Emerging Art & Sounds” that began with Monday-night sessions at Stone’s Throw Bar on Mannings Hill Road in Kingston.

By 2018, New Wave had moved to The Cove Restaurant & Bar on Winchester Road and widened the format beyond its early weekly showcase, making room for music, film, visual art, fashion and other creative programming.

Over time, it evolved into a broader Jamaican culture platform built around emerging creatives and community. Its programming expanded into pop-ups, special events and the New Wave Block Party — a larger celebration of music, food, art and entertainment that continued the same focus on new and current Jamaican talent.

This is where it started.
