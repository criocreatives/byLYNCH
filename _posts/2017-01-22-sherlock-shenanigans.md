I arrive at 8:07pm. Only seven minutes late but it seems I've missed a lot. They're already free-styling on table beats.

<figure><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1526" src="/assets/uploads/2017/01/img_6235.jpg" alt="IMG_6235.jpg" width="5999" height="3999"  loading="lazy" decoding="async"/><figcaption>Tron on the beat.</figcaption></figure>

Outside the Philip Sherlock building is where it's at. There's a show going on inside but it isn't time for these performers to go on. Warm up ting out here <em>fam</em>. Beat boxing and lyrical exchanges go on for minutes on end. The talent is strong.

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1541" src="/assets/uploads/2017/01/img_6263.jpg" alt="IMG_6263.jpg" width="5999" height="3999"  loading="lazy" decoding="async"/></figure>

Lyrics that contain explicit language and violent themes are often frowned upon; Based on the belief that these lyrics influence the youth to <em>do bad things</em>. Nonsense. Misguided youth <em>do bad things</em>.

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1608" src="/assets/uploads/2017/01/img_6337.jpg" alt="IMG_6337.jpg" width="5999" height="3999"  loading="lazy" decoding="async"/></figure>

The lyrical onslaught went on for over 30 minutes, featuring the stylings of Jeeby Lyricist, Chavez Illmatiq, Pag3y, Kenika Quash, &amp; Blvk H3ro. Here are the good bits:
