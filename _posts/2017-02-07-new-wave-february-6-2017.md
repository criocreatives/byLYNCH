It's February 6. A Monday. Bob Marley's <em>earthstrong. </em>New Wave is back.<i> </i>This season starts off with a performance from <a href="https://twitter.com/themeleku" target="_blank">Meleku</a>, son of Sizzla Kalonji.

<figure><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1904" src="/assets/uploads/2017/02/img_8202.jpg" alt="IMG_8202.jpg" width="5184" height="3456" /><figcaption>Meleku</figcaption></figure>

Jimmy Q, the resident DJ, is present along with the nights guest DJ's from BBC; <a href="http://seanib.com/" target="_blank">Seani B</a> and <a href="http://toddlat.com/" target="_blank">Toddla T</a>.

<figure><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1914" src="/assets/uploads/2017/02/img_82641.jpg" alt="IMG_8264.jpg" width="4076" height="3057" /><figcaption>From left: Seani B, Jimmy Q, Toddla T</figcaption></figure>

Bare vibes. Bare remixes. Bare tings. Popular faces rolling in. Photographers. Artistes. Producers. Collectives. Friends. Romans. Squad ting.
