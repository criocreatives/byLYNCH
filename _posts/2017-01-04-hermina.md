<p>Have you ever danced with the devil in the pale moonlight? I have.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/assets/uploads/2016/12/img_9525d.webp" alt="img_9525d" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="2000" height="1646"></figure>

<p>That "M" stands for "Mislead".</p>

<p>You embody the concept of deception. I don't know what's real. Camera trick. Your name is spelled differently on each of your bank accounts and your passport. Are you even real?</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/assets/uploads/2016/12/img_443921.webp" alt="img_44392" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="2000" height="750"></figure>

<p>I met you in the summer of 2016. We took a trip to the beach at midnight. We frolicked under the moon and I thought the rest of my life would be <em>lit</em>. My subconscious was watching out for me though; I ended <em>us </em>with good reason and with the intent of a rekindling. That never happened, thank God (a.k.a. Me).</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/assets/uploads/2016/12/img_4443.webp" alt="img_4443" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="2000" height="1333"></figure>

<p>I should've known you were no good. You slipped into my DM's on the 3rd. By the 12th I'm slipping into your <em>cooter cave</em>. Waddis? My fault still; I ignored the signs. There are always signs but you overlook them when you're enjoying the experience and being positive. You wrote a love letter on your ex-boyfriends wall and when I saw it you said he hacked your profile and did that. From dat mi a pree you.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="/assets/uploads/2016/12/img_9756-g.webp" alt="img_9756-g" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1964" height="2000"></figure>

<p>Ling Ling opened my eyes still. I'm glad we never got back together. I think your whole life is a lie. The school transcript you used in your job application was forged. You were telling people at work you lived alone. Why? I rented my second bedroom to you because you were <em>going through some shit</em>. You told Ling Ling I "just moved in last week" and it's your house. Bitch... The four months I let you stay here, you paid rent <strong>once. </strong>You didn't even pay all you owed. You were at work telling people you paid $15,000 every month. You told them you paid that $8,000 water bill all by yourself. Jezebel... You gimmi a gran fi throw pon it. Wow.</p>

<p>You're the worst person I've met in 2016. Hands down. I hope you trip and fall on your forehead. I hope you drop your phone in a drain. I hope your hair thins out. I hope you get fired. I hope you get some lame dick and catch an STD. Mi hope yuh foot bruk.</p>

<p>Life has its lessons still. I thought you were <a href="http://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Great%20White%20Buffalo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Great White Buffalo</a>. You turned out to be <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_sheep" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Black Sheep</a>. The only thing good about you was the p<em>w</em>ussy. Although you're always disabled after one round. Lame ass.</p>
